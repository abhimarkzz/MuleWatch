# IBM Plex

`IBMPlexSans-Variable-latin.woff2`, `IBMPlexMono-Regular-latin.woff2` and
`IBMPlexMono-Medium-latin.woff2` are Latin subsets of IBM Plex Sans and IBM
Plex Mono, copyright © 2017 IBM Corp. with Reserved Font Name "Plex".

They are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1:
https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

The files are vendored so the app builds and runs without contacting
Google Fonts.
